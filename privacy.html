Privacy

This is a translation for your convenience. The German version is the binding one.

In short: Brenda works on your Mac. Your conversations, projects and files stay there, not on our servers. Only what your task needs goes to the AI, and you can look up at any time what it was. We sell nothing, show no ads and don’t read along.

Who is responsible?

Martin Bender (Temvila), Revaler Straße 101, 10243 Berlin, Germany. Mail: hallo@temvila.com

This website

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). Like any web server, GitHub processes your IP address and technical details of the request to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. The site sets no cookies and counts nothing. In your browser it only remembers your language choice and, until you close the window, which step you were on. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (a safe, working site).

Your invitation comes by mail from hallo@temvila.com to the address you gave us yourself (or that the person who invited you passed on with your consent). We use it only to send you your invitation and to reach you during the test. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (you want to take part).

Your invitation is a personal code. It contains your mail address, so you recognise it in the app (“invited as …”), and how long Studio Pro lasts. The app checks it on your Mac; nothing is sent over the internet for it, not even a device id. The link in your mail carries the invitation after a “#”: that part stays in your browser and is not sent to our page; the page removes it from the address bar at once and hands it only to the app on your Mac with “Open Temvila”.

What stays on your Mac

Your studio, projects, conversations, results and files are kept in the folder “Documents › Brenda” and in the app’s folder on your Mac. What Brenda keeps about your work (for example conversation histories) is encrypted; the key lives in your Mac’s keychain, like all passwords and sign-ins. We have no access to it.

What goes to the AI

When you give Brenda a task, the text the AI needs for it goes to the AI provider:

  • “Brenda invites you” (our allowance): to Anthropic (USA). Usage runs through our account at Anthropic. There we only see the usage and cost of your access key, not your content. By its own rules, Anthropic does not train on content from its API. More in Anthropic’s privacy notice.
  • Your own AI: to Anthropic or OpenAI (USA), under your own contract with that provider.

Content from your accounts, such as mails and attachments, first passes a protective layer: account numbers, card numbers, tax and ID numbers, phone numbers, passwords and codes are replaced by placeholders (for example [IBAN_1]). The mapping stays encrypted on your Mac and never goes to the AI. In drafts and replies Brenda puts the real details back on your Mac. Names, addresses and amounts stay, because the work needs them.

“Please transfer to DE89 3704 0044 0532 0130 00, questions welcome at 0171 2345678.”
What the AI sees
“Please transfer to [IBAN_1], questions welcome at [TELEFON_1].”
In your draft
Brenda puts the real details back on your Mac before you see the draft.

What exactly reached the AI you can see at any time in the office under “What went to the AI?”. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (you gave Brenda the task).

Connections, when you switch them on

You need none for the test. If you connect a mailbox (for example Gmail, iCloud or GMX), Brenda reads only the folders you choose, saves replies only as drafts and sends nothing by herself. Your password or sign-in lives only in your Mac’s keychain. Brenda treats mail content as outside text: she is built not to follow instructions written in a mail. You can disconnect at any time.

When Brenda searches the web for you, your Mac fetches public pages directly; those pages see your IP address. Brenda sends no private details along and signs in nowhere.

Transfers to the USA

GitHub, Anthropic and OpenAI are based in the USA. Transfers there rely on the European Commission’s adequacy decision on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR) where the provider is certified under it, and otherwise on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses in the provider’s terms (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR). More at GitHub, Anthropic and OpenAI. Tally is based in the EU.

What we receive

  • Your feedback: only when you send it yourself (Settings › Feedback opens a ready mail to us).
  • Your invitation: your mail address and, if you told us, your first name (see above).
  • Your answers in the three surveys: they run through Tally (Tally BV, Belgium), without a name unless you write it in.
  • Nothing else. The app sends us no usage data by itself. It only checks GitHub now and then for a new version; GitHub sees your IP address, as with any website.

Legal basis for feedback and surveys: Art. 6(1)(a) and (f) GDPR (you send them voluntarily, we use them to improve Brenda).

How long

What is on your Mac stays until you delete it. We delete your sign-up, your mails to us and your survey answers when you ask us to, at the latest six months after the test ends. If your invitation came with an AI access key, we switch it off after the test.

Deleting

Move Temvila to the Trash and delete the folder “Documents › Brenda”. To be thorough, also delete “~/Library/Application Support/Brenda” and the Temvila and Brenda entries in your keychain.

Your rights

You can ask for information about your data, have it corrected, deleted or restricted, take it with you and object to its processing. You can withdraw consent at any time. Just write to the address above. You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example the one for your federal state.
